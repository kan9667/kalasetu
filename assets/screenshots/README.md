## Workflow Index & Screen Gallery

The screenshots in this folder map directly to the end-to-end artisan journey:

### 1. Onboarding & Authentication (`1. login/`)
* **1. login page.png** — Simple artisan login with mobile phone number and regional language switch.
* **2. new registration.png & 3. new registration-b.png** — Craft category, artisan cluster, and Pehchan card onboarding.
* **4. NGO coordinator.png** — Assisted digital onboarding mode for field coordinators and self-help groups (SHGs).
* **5. OTP verification screen.png** — Frictionless OTP authentication.
* **6. change lang.png** — Fast on-the-fly language selector across Indian regional languages.

### 2. Guided Interactive Walkthrough (`2.Walkthrough/`)
* **Overview.png & start listing.png** — Welcome visual introduction for first-time craftspeople.
* **step-1.png to step-6.png** — Step-by-step visual training modules explaining photo capture, voice narration, fair pricing, and order fulfillment.

### 3. Smart Catalog Management
* **3. my catalogue screen.png** — Live artisan inventory showcasing active, draft, and offline-queued craft listings.
* **11. product marked sold.png** — Real-time inventory status synchronization.

### 4. End-to-End Add Product Flow (`4. add product flow/`)
* **1. capture image.png & 2. review image.png** — Raw smartphone camera capture of handcrafted items.
* **3. record voice.png & 4. review-transcription.png** — Regional dialect narration transcribed with craft-domain vocabulary biasing.
* **5. enhancing image and listing generation.png** — Concurrent U²-Net studio background excision and bilingual title/description synthesis.
* **6. listing page.png & 13. list product page.png** — Structured bilingual (English + Hindi) listing preview with customer-facing SEO tags.
* **7. calculating fair price.png, 8. fair pricing suggestion.png, & 9. cost floor breakdown & market benchmarks.png** — Dual-layer fair pricing displaying the strict cost floor (`Materials + Labor × Fair Wage + Transport`) alongside indexed ChromaDB market comps.
* **10. review & publish.png** — Final artisan audit and sign-off before publishing.
* **11. social media launchpad.png & 12. social media msg generation.png** — One-tap marketing message generation for WhatsApp, Instagram, and Facebook.

### 5. KalaMitra Conversational AI Assistant (`5.chatbot-KalaMitra/`)
* **greetings-chatbot.png** — Multilingual voice & chat assistant greeting artisans in their chosen language.
* **change screen.png** — Direct in-app voice navigation executing screen changes.
* **mark as sold-chatbot.png** — Natural language voice action updating product catalog state.
* **change language.png** — Multilingual conversation switching.

### 6. Orders, Logistics & Packaging Advisory (`6. my orders/`)
* **1. orders page.png & 2. order details.png** — Real-time customer order management.
* **3. updating status.png** — Status progression from accepted to packaged and dispatched.
* **4. packaging guide.png** — Craft-specific eco-friendly packaging recommendations to prevent courier transit damage.
* **5. printable label.png** — Standardized shipping label generator with ONDC-compliant artisan profile QR code.

### 7. Artisan Analytics & Performance
* **7.1 artisan performance screen.png & 7.2 artisan performance screen.png** — Revenue analytics, top-selling crafts, catalog views, and fair wage earnings.

### 8. Profile & Utilities
* **8. my profile.png** — Master artisan credentials, Ministry of Textiles Pehchan ID, and craft cluster details.
* **9. change language.png** — Dedicated regional language preferences.
* **10. notifications.png** — Orders, policy scheme deadlines (e.g. PM Vishwakarma), and sync alerts.
* **12. sign out.png** — Secure session exit.
